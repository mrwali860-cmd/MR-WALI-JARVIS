## 3-minute demo script
**0:00–0:25 — The problem**
“Property enquiries can arrive while agents are busy. The operational issue isn't only writing a reply; it is capturing the lead, understanding intent, prioritizing follow-up, and keeping a reliable record.”

**0:25–1:05 — Submit a fictional lead**
“I'll enter a sample enquiry, industry, and contact details. This dashboard sends the form to the backend without refreshing the page. The backend validates the input and asks the AI to return a structured intent, HOT/WARM/COLD label, and response draft.”

**1:05–1:45 — Show the result and safety boundary**
“Here is the draft and priority label. This is a draft—not proof that a customer message was sent. The workflow can log it to Google Sheets and wait for an authorized human approval before sending.”

**1:45–2:20 — Show the record**
“We can inspect the lead record, draft, score, and delivery status. For a pilot, we will agree which source and channel to connect and how failed deliveries are flagged.”

**2:20–2:50 — Define measurable value**
“We will compare response-draft time, percentage of enquiries logged, time to human approval, failed delivery rate, and follow-up completion before and during the pilot. We won't claim additional deals unless the data supports it.”

**2:50–3:00 — Close**
“If this fits your process, let's choose one enquiry source and define a small paid pilot with a clear acceptance checklist.”

## Free prospecting workflow
1. Search public agency websites, LinkedIn company pages, and local property directories for agencies with active listings and a visible team.
2. Build a small list manually: agency, website, public business contact, relevant decision-maker, observed reason for fit, and contact date.
3. Prioritize agencies that clearly handle multiple enquiries or advertise several agents. Do not scrape private data or mass-message people.
4. Send a short, personalized email or LinkedIn note; follow the site's terms and applicable anti-spam/privacy laws. Respect opt-outs and platform limits.
5. Offer a demo using fictional or consented test data—not a real customer's private information.
6. Ask about current lead sources, response workflow, CRM, approvals, volume, and what outcome they would measure.
7. Send a scoped proposal only after confirming the problem, integrations, and acceptance criteria.
8. Track replies, demos booked, qualified opportunities, proposals, and paid pilots. Review results weekly.

## Pilot acceptance checklist
- [ ] Dashboard input reaches the backend and displays the result without a page refresh.
- [ ] Missing/invalid input is rejected with a useful error.
- [ ] AI failure is not reported as success.
- [ ] Lead and draft are logged to the agreed sheet/CRM.
- [ ] No email/WhatsApp is sent unless the approval field is true and an authorized process sets it.
- [ ] Test messages go only to a controlled test recipient.
- [ ] Delivery failures and timestamps are visible.
- [ ] Client signs off on data handling, scope, success metrics, and rollback plan.
